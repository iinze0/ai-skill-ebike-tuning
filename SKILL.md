# E-Bike Tuning & Conversion Skill

Practical guidance for modifying and building electric bikes.

## Core Workflow

1. Identify bike/motor system (e.g. Bosch Performance Line, Haibike HardSeven).
2. Diagnose issue or goal (breakage, speed limit, power increase).
3. Recommend approaches:
   - SpeedBox 3.0 or similar for Bosch bypass.
   - Motor swap or controller tuning.
   - Restrictor removal where applicable.
4. List parts, tools, and Norway-friendly suppliers/shipping.
5. Step-by-step install outline with safety warnings (warranty, legality of public road use).
6. Suggest testing protocol and torque specs.

## Safety Notes

- Modifications may void warranty and affect insurance/legality.
- High power systems need proper brakes, frame strength, and thermal management.
- Work in a well-ventilated area; follow electrical safety.

| System | Common Bypass | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|

| Shimano | Various chips | Check compatibility |
